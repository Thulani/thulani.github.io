About

I’m a senior software engineer with 20 years of experience building Java, Angular and Python applications for banking, insurance and telecoms, at organisations including Investec, Momentum, MTN and Rand Merchant Bank.

I trained in Computer Systems Engineering because I wanted to know how computers work from the hardware up. I’ve always liked taking systems apart to see how they work, and that curiosity turned into a career shipping production software end to end: Spring Boot services, Angular front-ends, the data layer and the CI/CD pipelines that deliver them.

I’m now growing towards software and solution architecture, and I’m exploring machine learning and how it will shape the systems we build next.

Away from the keyboard I keep up with new technology, play and watch football, try new restaurants and travel whenever I can.
